# Decision control and scope

- Read recorded `D` and `ADR` decisions before asking questions. Never reopen closed decisions without evidence of a new conflict.
- Product/business ambiguity → owner question with 2–3 options, consequences and recommendation; wait for explicit selection. Do not turn a recommendation into approved behavior.
- Technical choice within approved constraints → document rationale, alternatives, affected source-of-truth docs and ADR if significant.
- Expanding the scope requires new approval and plan impact assessment.
- Task completion is not permission to alter business rules, weaken DoD, or silently change user-facing behavior.
